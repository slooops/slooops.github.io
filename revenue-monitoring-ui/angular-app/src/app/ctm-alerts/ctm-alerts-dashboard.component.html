Alert Type Guide
FAILED

Job ended with a non-zero return code. CTM reported the job as Ended Not OK or Abended. Requires investigation of the job log to determine root cause.

DELAYED

Job is running longer than its p95 historical runtime. Still executing but has exceeded the normal completion window. May indicate a performance issue or data volume spike.

LATE_START

Job has not started by its expected start time (historical mean + buffer). No run of any status was found in CTM for that day — may indicate a scheduling or dependency issue.

ABEND

Job terminated abnormally — a system-level or unhandled failure (e.g. memory fault, signal kill). More severe than a standard failure and always escalated to P1.
